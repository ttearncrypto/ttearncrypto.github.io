Common questions

Everything about earning, learning and staying updated with crypto.

Is F9XR's TTEarnCrypto really free?

Yes. Every method we cover - faucets, airdrops and review networks - pays out without a deposit. You earn coins for time and attention, never for money in.

How do payouts work?

Coins land in your wallet automatically once you pass a method's minimum. Most faucets pay daily; airdrops pay out after listing.

What are the dashboards?

Trackers that pull live crypto prices and show what each earning method is worth today, so you can pick the highest-paying faucet at a glance.

Is my wallet safe with you?

We never hold your wallet. The address you enter is only used to route payouts to you, and demo entries on this page never leave your browser. See our privacy policy.

Where can I learn more about crypto?

Start with the crypto news hub and the dashboards, then follow X / Twitter and join the Discord and Telegram for daily updates, listing alerts and method walkthroughs.

How do I stay updated?

All of F9XR's TTEarnCrypto's community channels - X / Twitter, Twitch, Discord, Telegram and Pinterest - are linked in the footer. Drop a question in Discord any time and the team answers.

Still stuck? Email hello@f9xr.org or ask in Discord.
