Privacy

F9XR's TTEarnCrypto is a static site published on GitHub Pages. It does not operate its own analytics, tracking scripts or advertising cookies. The only third-party data transfers are the standard requests your browser makes to Google Fonts and to any external link you open, subject to those providers' own policies.

If you contact us by email, we keep your message and address only to respond to you. We never sell personal data. Crypto wallet addresses you enter in the demo form on the home page are never transmitted anywhere - the form is illustrative and runs entirely in your browser.
