Legal & contact

How TTEarnCrypto handles your data, what our terms say, and how to reach the F9XR team.

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Privacy

TTEarnCrypto is a static site published on GitHub Pages. It does not operate its own analytics, tracking scripts or advertising cookies. The only third-party data transfers are the standard requests your browser makes to Google Fonts and to any external link you open, subject to those providers' own policies.

If you contact us by email, we keep your message and address only to respond to you. We never sell personal data. Crypto wallet addresses you enter in the demo form on the home page are never transmitted anywhere — the form is illustrative and runs entirely in your browser.

Terms of service

Content on TTEarnCrypto is provided for information only. Earning methods, payouts and token values change frequently; always check the live dashboard or the method's own page before relying on a figure. We are not a wallet, exchange or financial adviser, and nothing on this site is an offer or solicitation to invest.

You use third-party faucets, airdrops and ad networks at your own discretion. We are not liable for losses arising from your use of third-party services. The site and its content are provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.

Cookies

This site sets no functional or marketing cookies itself. Fonts are loaded from Google; if Google Fonts sets cookies, they are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. External links (X, Telegram, GitHub) may set their own cookies on those platforms.

Contact

Questions, corrections or takedown requests: email hello@f9xr.org, or visit the F9XR website.
